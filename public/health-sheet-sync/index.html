Health Sheet Sync

A personal Google Apps Script tool that syncs strength-training and bodyweight entries from a private Google Sheet into the user's own Google Health account.

What it does

  • Reads workout and bodyweight data the user types into their own spreadsheet.
  • Creates and updates exercise and weight datapoints in the user's Google Health account.
  • Runs entirely under the user's own Google account; no data is shared with anyone else.

Data use

The application accesses Google Health data solely to write the user's own workout and bodyweight records on their behalf. It does not sell, transfer, or share this data, and uses it only to provide the sync feature described above.
